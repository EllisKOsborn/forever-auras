# Changelog

## Shadow Priest v1.3.0 (17 Nov 2026)
Bigger update, from research into Forever's shadow priest. Updates in place
(choose **Update**). Not tested in game yet.
- **Procs** left of the rotation: Spirit Tap and Inner Focus, only while up,
  with a gold glow.
- **Coloured feedback:** DoTs glow gold in their last 3 seconds and get a red
  border while missing; Mind Blast and Shadow Word: Death glow purple when
  ready during a fight; the Shadow Weaving number turns gold at 5 stacks.
- **Mind Flay ticks:** two lines on the cast bar while channelling.
- **Mana bar** with the percentage, under the cast bar.
- **Cooldown row** adds Vampiric Embrace and your race's priest spells
  (Feedback, Divine Grace, Desperate Prayer, Chastise, Starshards, Elune's
  Grace, Confounding Flash, Contingency Plan, Dark Sacrifice; only yours show).
- **Cleaner icons:** thin black borders (orange on reminders), less zoom,
  bigger timer numbers.

## Shadow Priest v1.2.1 (17 Nov 2026)
- Shadow Weaving counts only **your** stacks: in Forever it boosts only the
  priest who applied it (Blizzard's priest class deep dive).

## Shadow Priest v1.2.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- Renamed to **Forever Auras | Shadow Priest**: a standalone pack.
- **This version imports as a new group.** Delete the old one in `/fa`
  first (the group named after the old name), then import. Later versions
  update in place again.

## Shadow Priest v1.1.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- **Cast bar** under the rotation row, as wide as the row: spell name on the
  left, time left on the right, shown only while casting or channelling.
  Uses ForeverAuras' Cast trigger, which lets the game move the bar in combat.
  Not tested in game yet.
- The cooldown row moved down under the cast bar.

## Shadow Priest v1.0.0 (17 Nov 2026)
First version, not tested in game yet.
- Rotation row: Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague, Vampiric Embrace (your
  debuffs on the target, grey while missing), Mind Blast and Shadow Word:
  Death (grey while cooling down), Shadow Weaving stacks.
- Cooldown row (only while cooling down): Silence, Psychic Scream, Fade,
  Inner Focus.
- Reminders (only while missing): Shadowform, Inner Fire, Fortitude.
- Priests only; icons stay hidden until you know the spell.
