# Changelog

## Shadow Priest v1.2.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- Renamed to **Forever Auras | Shadow Priest**: a standalone pack.
- **This version imports as a new group.** Delete the old one in `/fa`
  first (the group named after the old name), then import. Later versions
  update in place again.

## Shadow Priest v1.1.0 (17 Nov 2026)
- **Cast bar** under the rotation row, as wide as the row: spell name on the
  left, time left on the right, shown only while casting or channelling.
  Uses ForeverAuras' Cast trigger, which lets the game move the bar in combat.
  Not tested in game yet.
- The cooldown row moved down under the cast bar.

## Shadow Priest v1.0.0 (17 Nov 2026)
First version, not tested in game yet.
- Rotation row: Shadow Word: Pain, Devouring Plague, Vampiric Embrace (your
  debuffs on the target, grey while missing), Mind Blast and Shadow Word:
  Death (grey while cooling down), Shadow Weaving stacks.
- Cooldown row (only while cooling down): Silence, Psychic Scream, Fade,
  Inner Focus.
- Reminders (only while missing): Shadowform, Inner Fire, Fortitude.
- Priests only; icons stay hidden until you know the spell.
